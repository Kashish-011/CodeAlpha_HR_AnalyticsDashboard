# HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report designed to analyze workforce trends, employee attrition, demographic distribution, and salary insights. The dashboard helps HR teams make data-driven decisions by providing clear visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs).

This project was developed as part of the **CodeAlpha Power BI Internship Program**.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition and workforce trends.
- Understand employee distribution across departments.
- Evaluate workforce demographics by gender and age group.
- Analyze salary distribution across job roles.
- Provide HR insights for better decision-making.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Employees
- Attrition Rate
- Attrition Count
- Average Age
- Average Salary
- Average Tenure

### Visualizations
- Gender Distribution
- Education Field Distribution
- Employees by Department
- Employee Count by Job Role
- Attrition by Department
- Employees by Age Group
- Average Salary by Job Role

### Interactive Filters
- Department Filter
- Gender Filter

## 📂 Dataset

**IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset**

The dataset contains employee-related information including:
- Employee Demographics
- Department Details
- Job Roles
- Salary Information
- Attrition Status
- Education Fields
- Age Groups

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- Research & Development has the highest employee count.
- Employee attrition is concentrated in specific departments.
- The majority of employees belong to the 30–40 age group.
- Manager and Research Director roles have the highest average salaries.
- Workforce distribution varies significantly across job roles and education fields.

---

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open the `.pbix` file in Power BI Desktop.
2. Use the Department and Gender slicers to filter data.
3. Explore KPIs and visualizations for HR insights.
4. Analyze employee trends and attrition patterns.
